Landscape & Space

How is the space organised? What determines where one can and cannot walk?

What does the arrangement of paths, walls, and thresholds suggest about how the space was intended to be used?

Sounds & Silences

What is audible? What is conspicuously absent?

Boundaries & Transitions

Where does one kind of place end and another begin? Is the transition abrupt, gradual, or disputed?

Historical Traces

What evidence of earlier use, alteration, or abandonment is visible? What is difficult to date?

Unexpected Discoveries

What was noticed that was not anticipated? What seemed out of place, misplaced, or difficult to explain?

Route & Deviation

How did the route actually taken differ from the route intended? What caused the difference?

Unresolved Questions

What was not answered? What requires a return visit?

The Field Card is designed to be printed on standard letter or A4 paper in portrait orientation. It may be carried folded to pocket size. Entries may be made in pencil. The Institute has no objection to using the reverse side for further observations, maps, or anything else that seems relevant at the time.

For the formal framework governing field observations, see the Regulations Governing the Conduct of Field Observations. For excursion classification, see Academic Affairs.
