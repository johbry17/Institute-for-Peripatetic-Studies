Collegium Itinerantium

Solvitur Ambulando

January 1, 2026

Dear [Recipient],

It is with due ceremony and genuine satisfaction that I write to confirm that the Institute for Peripatetic Studies has this day conferred upon [Recipient] the degree of Licentiate of Peripatetic Studies (L.P.S.).

This distinction is awarded in recognition of your engagement with the world through movement, observation, and the pursuit of place-based knowledge. The Institute takes the view that such engagement — wherever it has led you, however circuitously, and regardless of whether you arrived where you intended — constitutes a meaningful contribution to the peripatetic tradition.

Please receive your diploma and this letter as formal documentation of the distinction conferred. The Institute encourages you to display them, discuss them freely, and continue the ambulatory inquiry that earned them. The path ahead remains, as always, the more interesting direction.

The Institute notes, for completeness, that the Licentiate of Peripatetic Studies is a self-conferred, non-accredited ceremonial distinction. It does not qualify the holder for graduate study, professional licensure, or employment. It does qualify the holder for an enhanced sense of intellectual purpose when walking somewhere interesting, and the Institute considers this the more valuable endowment.

Yours sincerely,

Bryan Johns
