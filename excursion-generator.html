About the Generator

The Excursion Generator is a small institutional instrument. Its purpose is to provide a formally phrased scholarly justification for a walk that one was probably going to take anyway. The Institute takes the view that most worthwhile excursions are undertaken in this spirit: something prompts a departure, the walk produces observations, and the observations retrospectively justify the original impulse. The Generator simply makes the first step more official.

The objectives produced by the Generator are genuine peripatetic research questions. The Institute encourages users to take them seriously, carry a field card, and record what they find.

For the observation framework to carry into the field, see the Peripatetic Field Card. For the formal classification of excursion types, see Academic Affairs.
