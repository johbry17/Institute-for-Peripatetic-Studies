Mission

The Institute for Peripatetic Studies advances Peripatetics — the interdisciplinary study of place through movement. We hold that wandering, observation, and direct engagement with landscapes produce a form of knowledge that no desk can replicate, and that not every worthy journey requires a destination to justify itself.

A Welcome from the Director

The Institute for Peripatetic Studies arose from a recognition that moving through the world teaches things that remaining in one place cannot. A landscape encountered on foot is a different object of study than a landscape described in a text or observed from a window. The path itself is a kind of argument.

This institution exists to take that argument seriously — with appropriate ceremony. It is devoted to the study of places, journeys, and the knowledge that emerges from direct engagement with the world. Its methods include walking, observation, conversation, and honest curiosity. Its guiding motto, Solvitur Ambulando, is both its program and its answer to most objections.

You are welcome here, whatever road brought you.

Bryan Johns

Founding Director  ·  Doctor of Peripatetic Studies

From the Office of the Founding Director

The Institute Announces the Completion of Its Foundational Administrative Phase

The Institute has concluded the organizational procedures necessary to its initial operation — including the commissioning of a seal, the establishment of a motto, the ratification of the founding charter, and the conferral of its first doctorate — and is pleased to confirm that further activity may now proceed on a less procedural basis.

Notice Concerning the Proper Recognition of Unscheduled Excursions

The Institute reaffirms that an excursion need not have been planned in advance to constitute legitimate peripatetic inquiry. Observations made during an errand, a missed connection, or a deliberate departure from the intended route are accorded the same standing as those arising from formally initiated field work.

The Founding Director Reaffirms the Institute's Commitment to Continued Existence

In response to no particular inquiry, the Founding Director wishes to confirm that the Institute for Peripatetic Studies remains operational and committed to its mission. A formal Notice of Institutional Continuity has been issued and is available in the Institutional Archive.

Call for Papers: Places That Resist Easy Classification

The Institute invites contributions to its ongoing inquiry into places whose character cannot be adequately described by a single category: locations that are simultaneously transitional and settled, marginal and central, historically significant and practically overlooked. Submissions of any length will be considered. No deadline has been established, which the Institute regards as consistent with its general position on the matter.

Field Notes — From the Archive

The Epistemology of the Unnecessary Detour

A preliminary examination of what, precisely, is learned by taking a route that was not required. The paper addresses the question of whether knowledge acquired incidentally — during a deviation, a digression, or a return by an unfamiliar path — is different in kind from knowledge sought directly, and whether the difference matters.
