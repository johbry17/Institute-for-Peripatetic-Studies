Formal documents, declarations, and official instruments of the Institute

The following documents constitute the Institute's formal record of its principles, standards, and official positions. They are issued with appropriate seriousness and should be received in the same spirit.

IPS/DOC/001

Founding Charter

The constitutive document of the Institute. Establishes its name, mission, principles, governance, and authority to confer ceremonial distinctions. Anno Domini MMXXIV.

IPS/DOC/002

Office of Peripatetic Standards

An instrument establishing what qualifies as a legitimate excursion under the Institute's framework. Addresses intention, observation, deviation, and the status of the useful journey that fails to arrive.

IPS/DOC/003

The Institute's Official Position on Detours

A formal declaration distinguishing the accidental deviation, the deliberate detour, and the route whose value becomes apparent only retrospectively. Issued in the interest of conceptual clarity.

IPS/DOC/004

Regulations Governing the Conduct of Field Observations

Five regulations concerning the recording of observations, the distinction between evidence and inference, the acknowledgment of uncertainty, and the documentation of departures from the intended route.

IPS/DOC/005

Notice of Institutional Continuity

A formal notice confirming that the Institute remains operational, committed to its mission, and available for the conduct of peripatetic inquiry. Issued in response to no particular inquiry.

IPS/DOC/006

D.P.S. Diploma — Bryan Johns

The founding doctorate of the Institute, conferred upon its Founding Director at the time of establishment. Printable and exportable as PDF or PNG.

IPS/DOC/007

D.P.S. Instrument of Conferral

The formal record of the conferral of the Doctor of Peripatetic Studies upon Bryan Johns, Founding Director. A separate institutional record distinct from the diploma itself.
