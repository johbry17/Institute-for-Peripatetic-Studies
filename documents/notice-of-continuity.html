Notice of Institutional Continuity

A Formal Confirmation of Ongoing Operations

The Institute for Peripatetic Studies, Collegium Itinerantium, hereby issues formal notice of its continued operational status. This notice is issued in the interest of institutional transparency and in accordance with the Institute's general commitment to documenting its activities, however administrative in character those activities may be.

§ 1 — Confirmation of Existence

The Institute for Peripatetic Studies remains in existence. Its founding charter remains in force. Its seal continues to be the authoritative emblem of the institution. Its motto, Solvitur Ambulando, remains both its guiding principle and its answer to most objections.

The Institute is aware that its continued existence was not in serious doubt and that this notice is therefore, in the ordinary sense of the term, unnecessary. The Institute nonetheless considers it useful to confirm these facts formally, on the grounds that formal confirmation of what is already known is among the more underrated activities of a functioning institution.

§ 2 — Confirmation of Mission

The Institute's mission — to advance the interdisciplinary study of place through movement, to formalize the methods of peripatetic inquiry, and to confer upon its practitioners the institutional recognition they have long merited — remains unchanged and is actively pursued.

Field research is ongoing. The Field Notes archive continues to receive entries, though not on any fixed schedule, which the Institute regards as consistent with the character of the work. The diploma generator is operational. The founding charter and all derivative institutional documents remain available for review.

§ 3 — Confirmation of Academic Distinctions

The two distinctions established by the Institute at its founding — the Doctor of Peripatetic Studies (D.P.S.) and the Licentiate of Peripatetic Studies (L.P.S.) — remain valid and continue to be recognized by the Institute as genuine, if entirely self-conferred, marks of distinction.

Holders of these distinctions are encouraged to continue their peripatetic activities, to record their observations, and to maintain the independent relationship with the path that the Institute's academic regulations commend.

§ 4 — A Note on the Nature of This Notice

The Institute acknowledges that a notice confirming one's own continued existence occupies a somewhat unusual position in the range of institutional communications. It is not a response to a closure. It is not a relaunch. It is, simply, a formal record of the fact that the Institute is here, that it is doing what it said it would do, and that it expects to continue doing so.

The Institute has issued this notice because it believes that stating ordinary things formally is, occasionally, a worthwhile activity. The world contains a great deal of undocumented continuity. The Institute has chosen to document some of it.

Solvitur ambulando.

Issued under the authority of the Institute for Peripatetic Studies, Collegium Itinerantium. The Institute confirms that this notice was drafted, reviewed, and issued on the same day, a circumstance the Founding Director considers a model of administrative efficiency. Anno Domini MMXXIV.
