Institute for Peripatetic Studies

Collegium Itinerantium

Be it known to all persons of learning and goodwill

The Institute for Peripatetic Studies, Collegium Itinerantium, by the authority vested in its Founding Director, and in recognition of the foundational establishment of Peripatetics as a formal field of intellectual inquiry, of sustained engagement with landscapes through movement and observation, and of abiding conviction that not every journey requires a destination, does hereby confer upon

Bryan Johns

the degree of

Doctor of Peripatetic Studies

(D.P.S.)

with all the rights, privileges, and wandering liberties pertaining thereunto, including the full authority to establish further institutional distinctions, confer them upon others, and advance the peripatetic tradition by whatever ambulatory means he judges appropriate.

Given under our hand and seal

the First Day of October,
in the Year Two Thousand and Twenty-Four

Solvitur Ambulando

Bryan Johns

Founding Director

Institute for Peripatetic Studies
